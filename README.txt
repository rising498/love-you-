OUR LITTLE LOVE STORY 💜

Included:
- index.html (the website)
- assets/ (your 4 uploaded photos)

Preview:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.

Publish with Netlify:
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in if asked.
3. Drag the extracted folder named "our_love_website" onto the deploy area.
4. Netlify will create a shareable website link.

Privacy note:
A normal Netlify link is accessible to anyone who has the link, and it may be discoverable depending on settings. Don't include private details you wouldn't want others to see.
