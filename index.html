En uyire, ❤️

Enna manichiru. Naan pannina mistakes unakku hurt aagirundha, adha naan light-ah eduthukka maaten. Sorry nu solradhu mattum podhaadhu nu enakku theriyum; inimel en actions-la adha kaamikkanum. Un feelings-a nalla purinjikka try pannuven, kovathula pesama konjam yosichu pesuven, unakku thevaiyana nerathula un pakkathula iruppen.

Nee illama en life-a imagine pannave kashtama irukku. Aana un mela irukkura love-na, unna happy-ah feel panna vechittu, un feelings-kum space kudukkanum nu enakku theriyum. Namma rendu perum mistakes-la irundhu learn panni, innum nalla understand pannikkalaam. 🫂

Namma photos-a paaru… first photo-la namma cute-ah irukkom; next photo-la namma glow vera level; andha cartoon photo-la namma rendu perum baby versions maadhiri! 😂💗 Namma serndha nerathula irundha happiness, namma sirippu, namma chinna chinna memories — idhellam enakku romba precious.

Unna ovvoru naalum perfect-ah love panna try pannuven. Promise-na words mattum illa; patience, respect, honesty, care ellathulayum kaamikkanum. Namma rendu perum happy-ah, safe-ah, manasula edhu irundhaalum open-ah pesura relationship build pannalaam. 💜

I love you so much, di. Nee enakku romba special. Innaikku mattum illa, daily unnai choose panna virumburen. 💋🌷
